# MirrorFly

> MirrorFly is the #1 self-hosted CPaaS provider to integrate AI agent, chat, video, contact center, VoIP, & Chatbot SDKs into any web & app in just 24 hours.

Key capabilities and important notes:

- Self-hosted and on-premises deployment for complete data control
- AI-powered agents, chatbots, and conversational AI built-in
- Enterprise-grade real-time chat, voice, and video APIs
- Multi-platform SDKs for Web, iOS, Android, and Desktop
- Contact center and customer service automation capabilities
- White-label solutions for partners and resellers
